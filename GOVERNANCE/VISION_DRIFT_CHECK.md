# Vision Drift Check

Every Stage 0 review must record answers to:

- Does this advance the Strategic Compass and this generation's defining capability?
- What important scope is deferred, and why is the justification strong?
- Is the generation shrinking below its intended capability?
- Is safety preserved without removing usefulness?
- What observable end-to-end value loop will run?
- Is a new generation number actually necessary?
- Which E0–E6 milestone advances?
- Are inherited blockers explicitly tracked rather than renamed limitations?

Approval is prohibited until each answer is evidenced in the handoff and traceability record.
