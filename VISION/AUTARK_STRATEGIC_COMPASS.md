# Autark Strategic Compass

This is the canonical strategic vision. Engineering generations refine the original phases; they do not replace them.

## Original phases

1. **Birth / Genesis:** deterministic organism with constitution, identity, wallet, ledger, sandbox, memory, planning, budget, policy, execution, events, capabilities and security.
2. **Perception:** continuous, provenance-bearing observation of the world; accepted observations become memory.
3. **Memory:** short/working/long-term, semantic, episodic, skill, business, financial, failure and lesson memory.
4. **Learning:** reinforce successful strategies, forget weak ones, extract skills, improve planning/budgeting/scheduling and evaluate hypotheses.
5. **Dreaming:** offline replay, simulation, concept synthesis and hypothesis generation with no real-world authority.
6. **Self-Evolution:** evidence-backed proposals, simulation, constitutional/security/economic/regression verification, approval and rollback.
7. **Autonomous Business:** opportunity discovery, product creation, deployment, operation, market/support workflows, earning and reinvestment.
8. **Collective Intelligence:** specialized organisms with shared communication, constitution, economy, goals and knowledge.
9. **Artificial Life:** self-maintaining, observing, learning, repairing, documenting, financing and constitutionally controlled replication.

## Canonical generation mapping

| Generation | Capability transition |
|---|---|
| Gen-0 | Genesis/body: deterministic execution, identity, policy, capabilities, security, Treasury/wallet architecture, events and recovery |
| Gen-1 | Mind: memory, cognition, reflection and offline dreaming foundations |
| Gen-2 | Instinct: needs, drives, motivation, curiosity and homeostasis |
| Gen-2A | Perception and learning completion: observation, evidence-bound learning, forgetting and hypotheses |
| Gen-3 | Builder intelligence: research, opportunity evaluation, planning, creativity and tested artifacts |
| Gen-4 | Operator and temporal agency: persistent multi-day missions, deployment, supervision, maintenance, recovery and bounded real APIs |
| Gen-5 | Autonomous enterprise: customers, communication, pricing, sales, contracts, revenue, portfolio and reinvestment |
| Gen-6 | Autark family/society: multiple specialized organisms and collective intelligence |
| Gen-7 | Autogenesis/governed evolution: simulation, testing, constitutional review, independent verification and rollback |

Beyond Gen-7, human sovereignty, constitution, auditability and protected boundaries are permanent.

## Value milestones

E0 foundational/no economic claim; E1 useful artifact; E2 operated value; E3 first assisted dollar; E4 first autonomous dollar; E5 repeatable revenue; E6 self-financing. Gen-4 targets E2 and may reach E3 with human commercial assistance. Gen-5 targets E4/E5. Gen-6+ cannot substitute for credible Gen-5 real-value evidence.

## Gen-4 defining loop

Useful artifact → persistent mission → approved deployment → health verification → observation → bounded maintenance → recovery/rollback → measured outcome → learning feedback. Temporal hierarchy is Mission → Phase → Milestone → Task → Action proposal, with deadlines, dependencies, checkpoints, waits/wake conditions, budgets, retries, escalation, deterministic resume, drift detection, cancellation and completion proof. Planning never bypasses Policy, Treasury, Approval, ProtectedActionGateway or autonomy envelopes.
